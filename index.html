About NAMI Southern Nevada

Understanding the organization and the purpose behind its work.

NAMI Southern Nevada is a local affiliate of the National Alliance on Mental Illness, dedicated to improving the lives of individuals and families affected by mental illness.

Since 1979, the organization has worked throughout Southern Nevada to provide free mental health education, peer support, and advocacy.

Through community involvement and lived experience, NAMI strives to create an environment where people feel understood, supported, and empowered.

Our Mission

To provide support, education, and advocacy that improves the lives of people affected by mental illness in Southern Nevada.

Why Mental Health Matters

Building awareness is an important step toward a more understanding community.

Mental health is an important part of overall well-being. Yet misunderstandings and stigma can make it difficult for individuals and families to feel comfortable discussing their experiences.

Organizations like NAMI help change this by encouraging education, open conversations, and greater community involvement.

Understanding

Education helps replace misconceptions with knowledge and compassion.

Acceptance

Reducing stigma helps create a community where people feel respected.

Connection

Community involvement reminds individuals and families that they are not alone.

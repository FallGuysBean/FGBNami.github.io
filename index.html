Our Purpose

Creating a community where mental health matters.

NAMI Southern Nevada is dedicated to improving the lives of individuals and families affected by mental illness. Through free support, education, and advocacy, NAMI works to reduce stigma and create a compassionate community where people can find hope and understanding.

Our Community Efforts

Working together to make mental health support accessible to everyone.

🤝

Peer Support

Providing support groups where individuals and families can connect, share experiences, and find understanding.

📚

Mental Health Education

Offering free educational programs that help people understand mental illness, recovery, and how to support loved ones.

📢

Advocacy

Raising awareness, challenging stigma, and advocating for better mental health services and policies.

🌱

Youth Mental Health

Supporting young people through mental health awareness presentations, youth support groups, and community resources.

🏘️

Community Outreach

Connecting with schools, families, organizations, and local communities to spread awareness and encourage understanding.

💜

Hope and Recovery

Encouraging recovery, promoting wellness, and reminding individuals that their experiences matter.

Be Part of the Change

Mental health affects everyone. By volunteering, participating in community events, sharing resources, and supporting mental health awareness, we can help create a more inclusive and understanding Southern Nevada.
